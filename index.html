About

The Artist

I'm Saqib Ali, a freelance 3D artist based in Faisalabad, Pakistan, working full-time in Blender. My work sits at two ends of the same discipline — photorealistic architectural visualization and low-poly game asset creation.

On the visualization side, I craft renders that communicate the true feel of a space before it's built — from warm bedroom lighting to crisp corporate environments. Every material, shadow, and composition is deliberate.

On the game side, I build performance-first 3D assets: environments, vehicles, buildings, and props with clean topology, game-engine-ready UV maps, and proper polygon budgets. Delivered in FBX, OBJ, or BLEND — ready to drop straight into your pipeline.
